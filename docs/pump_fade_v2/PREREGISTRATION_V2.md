# Pump-fade v2 causal preregistration — engineering freeze candidate

**Source authority:** conversation's shared task contract. **Rule authority:** `config/pump_fade_v2/policy_v2.json`. **Status:** engineering preregistration; NOT a valid independent efficacy registration until source/data chronology and hash manifest are verified. Do not change thresholds after examining labels. Revisions require a new policy ID, an explicit exposure entry and a new sample.

The parent opportunity is one symbol/event at the close of a fully closed 5-minute Binance USD-M kline. A candidate pump requires `close_t / close_{t-288} - 1 >= 0.30` and trailing 288 bars' quote volume >= 2m USDT; continuity and a point-in-time eligible active USD-M symbol snapshot are mandatory. A rolling 60-minute same-symbol cooldown admits the first event and excludes later duplicates even if the price remains high. Reference prices and all 1h/4h returns use closed bars only; no present-day survivors are retrospectively inserted. Event time is the last closed bar's close-time, while decision time must be >= the actual receipt/availability time of every contributing input. Multiple symbols share days; overlapping events retain a single parent ID and do not become multiple independent tests. Dataset gaps and market exit censor opportunities.

State starts WAIT at event admission. State precedence each evaluation is terminal SKIP or EXPIRED; then input UNAVAILABLE blocks candidate; then WAIT with continuation-risk overlay; else FADE-CANDIDATE when any *separately measured* R1–R5 is verified. WAIT is true if trailing fully closed 4h return >15%, trailing 1h return >5%, last fully closed 1h quote volume >=2 × median of the previous 12 disjoint full hours, or a point-in-time **observed** funding cap hit/interval shortening in the last 24h. Missing mandatory 1h, 4h, volume-reference, cap history, OI, depth or BBO stays WAIT_UNAVAILABLE. An observed new high beyond the last observable peak, OI increase >=10% against a point-in-time event snapshot, or forced BUY short liquidation gives CONTINUATION-RISK overlay while WAIT. Force liquidation is censored exchange sampling: absence has no negative meaning. SKIP overrides WAIT, remains absorbing per event, with no second entry in the episode. Expiry is event time +24h, evaluated at receipt-time, and no reentry before expiry.

**Quality/SKIP:** listing age <7 full days at observed listing-time snapshot; spread >30 bps; aggregate executable-depth notional within 50bps <100,000 USDT on either side; BBO/depth >5s stale; skip primary joint post-peak drawdown >=30% and trough-to-current rebound >=10%. The other eight 20/30/40 × 5/10/15 joint cells and two inconsistent prose interpretations are separate arms. An unknown quality input inhibits release, not a pass.

**Release R1:** run a strictly observable high watermark from event onward and accept 60 minutes with no higher subsequent fully closed 5m high; reset waiting timer on a new high. **R2:** fully closed 15m low breaks the minimum lows of the two preceding *closed* 15m bars; within the next four fully closed 15m bars, a retest high reaches within 0.25% of the break price, but its close is below the break level and high stays below the event-to-date high; no future bar is accessed. **R3:** latest eligible *lagged* OI snapshot declines >=5% from the observable event-to-date peak; exclude the last completed row as a one-row publication lag and require each earlier sampled row's actual receipt time <= decision time and <=15m stale. **R4:** the last fully closed 15m close is below AVWAP computed from base volume and typical prices of closed 5m bars beginning at the first fully closed post-event bar (fixed anchor). **R5:** after an observed near-cap predicted funding rate (`cap - rate <= 0.0001`), three chronologically observed predicted funding samples each spaced >=60 seconds show `rate <= cap - 0.001` under the cap metadata observed at that time. Settled funding is never substituted for predicted. Unavailable metadata means unavailable candidate. Each R1–R5 is assessed alone before selecting pairs.

**Manual ladder:** freeze initial quantity q0, invalidation I and USDT risk budget R on first fill. For shorts I must exceed every approved price; for longs I must be below every approved price. Maximum two **risk-increasing** additions, each <=0.5 q0, final filled quantity <=2 q0. Every addition requires new release confirmation and WAIT/SKIP/CONTINUATION-RISK blocks it. Reserve conservative 30bps round-trip cost on each admitted or proposed fill: `Σ q_i (|I - P_i| + 0.003 P_i) <= R`. Partial fills aggregate toward both capacity and budget. Execution count and additions count differ; do not calibrate P3 until manual fills are reconciled. The alternative three-add policy is a registered sensitivity. Exact LIQ requires user-provided side-aware liquidation price and margin/mark context; otherwise UNKNOWN.

**Outcomes and cost:** event and alert origins tracked separately at 4h and 24h; 20% adverse mark-price squeeze, mark MAE, net executable short returns, registered 14/20/25/33% mark-distance near-LIQ scenarios, structural triple barriers and median missed fade threshold 10% favorable move. Use executable bid/ask or defensible scenario fills, documented fees, actual timestamped funding settlement, slippage, nonfill and opportunity costs. BBO has no queue priority; passive maker nonfills are UNKNOWN without trades/depth queue evidence. When executable inputs are unavailable output UNAVAILABLE rather than pretending mark is executable. Report per **original admitted event**, including skipped, expired, never-released and pending episodes, before conditional per-trade results. Same initial R and initial exposure for ladder comparisons; baseline ladder uses eight equal increments every +5% from last fill, frozen stop and risk normalization, with infeasible proposals rejected.

**Comparison families, no posthoc relaxation:** R1 five releases vs immediate same-parent cohort: squeeze-rate ratio <=0.75, median per-day missed-fade fraction <=0.50 (non-released events with >=10% beneficial price fall / all such events). R2 bounded ladder vs first-entry-only and 8×5%-spaced baseline: 24h original-event q99 **positive loss tail** reduction >=40% and net value noninferior by no more than 10 basis points; if too few clusters, descriptive only. R3 nine joint + two separately named prose sensitivity arms: 24h post-event squeeze risk ratio (skipped/non-skipped) >=1.5, which is association, not proof skip improves profitability. R4 continuation-long: offline refutation only, no live long. Specify parent-level paired differences, initial notional-normalized bps and 95% percentile CIs. Use day-block bootstrap 10,000 draws, seed 20261007, assigning entire overlapping episode to original UTC event day and resampling all symbols in that day together. Correct p-values within R1(5), R2(2), R3(11), R4(1) using Holm; do not infer independent trials from rows sharing dates. For q99 and rare squeezes, report cluster count/power and avoid claimed significance without adequate tail events. No fixed sample of 150 automatically proves power.

Require 95% one-sided cluster-bootstrap lower confidence bound of forward **net per-original-event value >0**, positive relative value against preregistered baseline, and tail bounds meeting the frozen P1 90% interval comparison (after valid sample).
