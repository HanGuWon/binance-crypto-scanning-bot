## Event, WAIT and releases

One parent per symbol's closed 5m event with `close / close_288_bars_ago - 1 >= 30%`, trailing 288 closed 5m quote volume >=2m USDT, and 60m symbol cooldown. Original parent denominator includes WAIT, never-released, SKIP, EXPIRED and censored records. Event expires 24h after event. A WAIT: fully closed **4h return >15%**. B WAIT: fully closed **1h return >5%**. D WAIT primary: last fully closed 1h quote volume **>=2x arithmetic mean of preceding 24 complete nonoverlapping 1h quote-volume bars**; sensitivity D12: >=2x median of preceding 12 complete nonoverlapping hours (v2 legacy median is deliberately **not** primary v3). Funding cap touched or observed interval shortened during prior 24h is also WAIT. CONTINUATION-RISK overlay: new observed high, PIT OI increase >=10% from event snapshot, or sampled forced BUY/short liquidation. Precedence: absorbing SKIP/EXPIRED, missing/unavailable, WAIT/continuation overlay, then FADE-CANDIDATE.

Independent preregistered release hypotheses: **R1** 60m without higher closed 5m high (timer resets on new high); **R2** fully closed 15m low under preceding two closed 15m lows then failed retest within next four closed 15m bars (high within 0.25% of break, close below break, high below event high); **R3** lagged eligible observed OI falls >=5% from observable event-to-date peak, excluding newest row and requiring earlier actual receipt <=decision and staleness <=15m, with event baseline <=5m; **R4** fully closed 15m close below AVWAP anchored to first fully closed post-event 5m bar, typical-price/base-volume weighting; **R5** cap-near predicted funding followed by three observed normalized predicted rates spaced >=60s, with intact point-in-time cap/interval; historical R5 **UNTESTABLE**, not false, because prior cap/interval series unavailable. Combinations require a new prefreeze; never select retrospectively.
